Our purpose

Giving kids a chance to just be kids

At Royal Family KIDS Camp, adult Camp Counselors give attention and encouragement to their campers all week long. Each camper gets to try a variety of fun activities, designed for their success and to build self-esteem.

Camp is a special time when children who have faced hard things can focus on having fun, feel safe, and know they are loved.

Safe by design

Every detail is planned with the children's safety in mind

  • Directors, staff, and counselors go through intensive training, including a study of child abuse and neglect led by recognized professionals.
  • Every counselor and staff member goes through an intensive screening process.
  • Each counselor is assigned two children, and only two, with a back-up staff person nearby.
  • Strict behavior policies and a campsite chosen to be safe and fun.
